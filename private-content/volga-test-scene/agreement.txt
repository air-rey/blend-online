PART A — CONFIDENTIALITY

1.  Confidential Information. The Parties may have access to marketing, financial, technical, and other information, ideas, concepts, materials, strategies and plans relating to the other Party (the “Disclosing Party”) and the Disclosing Party’s clients (including former clients and prospective clients), that is non-public, confidential or proprietary in nature (collectively, “Confidential Information”). To be clear, all Work Product (as defined in Section 5), whether created by the Contractor, the Company or both, is the Company’s Confidential Information from the moment it is created, with the Company treated as the Disclosing Party and the Contractor as the Receiving Party, unless and until the Company publicly releases it. The Company’s Confidential Information also specifically includes the Brief (as defined in Exhibit A), the Company’s scenarios, hazard sequences, user flows, observation and interaction logic, scoring and analytics methods, session data structures, art direction, Company-supplied assets, and the identity of the Company’s clients and prospective clients. Exclusions (iii) and (iv) below do not apply to Work Product. Confidential Information does not include any of the foregoing items for which the non-Disclosing Party (the “Receiving Party”) can demonstrate (i) has been approved for release by written authorization of the Disclosing Party; (ii) is or becomes part of information that is publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (iii) was known by the Receiving Party prior to the disclosure thereof by the Disclosing Party; (iv) was independently developed by employees or agents of the Receiving Party without reference to or use of any Confidential Information communicated to the Receiving Party by the Disclosing Party; or (v) properly comes into the possession of the Receiving Party from a third party that is not under any obligation to maintain the confidentiality of such information. The Receiving Party hereby acknowledges that all Confidential Information is and will remain the sole property of the Disclosing Party.

2.  Non-Disclosure. The Receiving Party shall hold all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in the strictest confidence, shall not disclose any Confidential Information (including, without limitation, via text message, blogging, or communicating in any way via Twitter, Facebook, or any other social networking website, tool, or device, etc.), shall not remove any copyright notice, trademark notice, and/or other proprietary legend set forth on or contained within any of the Confidential Information, and neither Party shall, at any time, disclose the nature or content of any Confidential Information belonging to the Disclosing Party to a third party unless the disclosure is authorized in writing by the Disclosing Party. In addition, Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information pursuant to a judicial or other government order, provided that Receiving Party shall provide Disclosing Party with prompt notice prior to any disclosure so that the Disclosing Party may seek other legal remedies to maintain the confidentiality of such Confidential Information, and Receiving Party shall comply with any applicable protective order or equivalent.

3.  Use Restrictions. Both parties agree that they shall not at any time copy or use any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, except to the extent authorized by the other Party. The Receiving Party shall only disclose Confidential Information to those of its employees and independent contractors that have a need to know such information, and the Receiving Party agrees to communicate to any employees and independent contractors to whom the Receiving Party furnishes Confidential Information the confidential nature of such information and the confidentiality obligations set forth in this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement that is caused by any of its employees or independent contractors, and the Receiving Party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party and its clients from and against any liabilities, claims, damages, losses, costs and expenses (including, without limitation, reasonable attorneys’ fees) resulting, directly or indirectly, from any breach by the Receiving Party, or any of its employees or independent contractors, of any provision of this Agreement.

4.  Return of Materials; Term. Upon the termination of the Term (as defined below), or at any time upon the request of either party or any of their clients, the Receiving Party shall promptly return all copies of Confidential Information which are in its possession or control, including any copies, notes or extracts prepared by the Receiving Party. This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years unless terminated earlier by either Party on seven (7) days’ written notice (the “Term”). The Parties’ obligations with respect to Confidential Information that became known to a Receiving Party during the Term continue for three (3) years after the end of the Term, except that obligations relating to Work Product, and to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret, continue for as long as that information remains confidential. Part B and Sections 17, 20 and 21 survive the end of the Term.

6.  Assignment of Work Product. The Contractor hereby assigns, and agrees to assign, to the Company all right, title and interest in and to the Work Product and all Intellectual Property Rights therein. The assignment is worldwide, perpetual (for the full term of the Intellectual Property Rights, including all renewals and extensions), and covers all modes of exploitation. The Parties expressly agree that the assignment is not subject to any default rule under Section 19 of the Copyright Act, 1957 (India), including the default five-year term, the default limitation of the assignment to the territory of India, and the lapse of an assignment where the rights are not utilized within one year. No royalty is payable for this assignment. In accordance with Section 19(3) of the Copyright Act, 1957, the consideration for it is set out in Section 16. To the extent moral rights cannot be assigned under applicable law, the Contractor waives, and agrees not to assert, such rights against the Company or its clients with respect to the Work Product.

7.  Background IP. Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Contractor’s use of its general skills, knowledge and experience.

8.  Disclosure and Further Assurances. The Contractor shall promptly disclose all Work Product to the Company and, at the Company’s request and expense, shall sign such documents and take such actions as are reasonably necessary to perfect, register or enforce the Company’s rights in the Work Product.

9.  Delivery of Source Materials. The Work Product includes, and the Contractor shall deliver to the Company in the manner set out in Exhibit A, all source materials needed to open, modify and rebuild it, including the complete Unreal Engine project, source code, Blueprints, assets, materials, configuration and build files, and the full version-control repository with its history and large-file storage objects. Delivery of a compiled build (such as an APK) alone does not satisfy this obligation. Delivery is not a condition of the assignment in Section 6, which takes effect on creation.

10.  Contractor Personnel. The Contractor warrants that each of its employees, agents and subcontractors who perform any part of the Services is or will be bound by a written agreement under which all work product of their engagement is assigned, or is required to be assigned, to the Contractor, so that the Contractor can and does validly assign all such rights to the Company under this Agreement.

11.  Originality and Third-Party Materials. The Contractor warrants that the Work Product is original to the Contractor or its personnel, except for (a) materials supplied by the Company and (b) third-party materials (including assets from Fab, Quixel Megascans or similar sources) that the Contractor identifies to the Company in writing, with each item’s name, source and licence, before delivery. To the Contractor’s knowledge, the Work Product does not infringe the rights of any third party. The Contractor shall use materials supplied by the Company, including third-party assets licensed to the Company (such as Fab or Quixel Megascans assets), only to perform the Services and in compliance with their licence terms, shall not share, resell or reuse them for any other purpose, and shall delete all copies of them on completion of the Services or earlier on the Company’s request.

12.  Portfolio Use. The Contractor shall not display, publish, or otherwise use the Work Product or any Confidential Information in any portfolio, website, case study, social media post or other promotional material, without the Company’s prior written consent, which the Company may grant or withhold in its sole discretion.

13.  Non-Replication. During the Term and for three (3) years after the end of the Term, the Contractor shall not, whether for itself or for any third party, use the Work Product, the Brief or any of the Company’s Confidential Information, in whole or in part, to create any scene, product or experience, including by replicating or adapting the Company’s scenarios, hazard sequences, user flows, observation and cue logic, or distinctive visual treatment (including the observation-cue highlight pulse described in the Brief), except as expressly authorized by the Company in writing. 14.  Non-Circumvention. During the Term and for three (3) years after the end of the Term, the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, contact or do business with any client, prospective client or lead of the Company that was introduced to the Contractor by the Company or of which the Contractor became aware through the Services, for the purpose of providing services that compete with the Company’s business, or otherwise circumvent the Company in any transaction or business opportunity with any such party.

15.  Deletion of Source Files and Repositories. Upon the Company’s written request, the Contractor shall, within seven (7) days, permanently delete all source files, repositories, builds and other copies of the Work Product and Confidential Information in its possession or control, except to the extent retention is required by applicable law, and shall provide the Company with a written certification, signed by an authorized representative of the Contractor, confirming such deletion.

EXHIBIT A: SERVICES

The Services consist of an unpaid test engagement to design and develop a single VR test scene (the “Test Scene”) for the Company, as specified in the Company’s written brief titled “BlendXR x Volga: Test Scene Brief”, dated 29 October 2026 and made available to the Contractor through the Company’s access-controlled website, together with any changes the Parties agree in writing, including by email (the “Brief”). The Brief is incorporated into this Exhibit by reference. In summary:

(a)  Scope: one Unreal Engine scene of about 25 to 45 seconds for Meta Quest 3, using hand tracking, head-gaze observation logic and session event logging, meeting the visual, audio, performance and project standards set out in the Brief.

(b)  Timeline: fourteen (14) days from the Contractor’s written confirmation that it can access all Company-supplied assets, with checkpoints on day 4 (gray box capture video), day 9 (first APK) and day 14 (final delivery).

(c)  Final deliverables: an optimized, sideloadable Quest 3 APK; the complete project as one archive of the full Git repository, including the .git folder, its full history and all Git LFS objects, which the Company can open, modify and rebuild using only the README; the README described in the Brief, including a list of every third-party asset with its source and licence; and one sample session data file.

(d)  Delivery: through the upload function on the Company’s access-controlled website, or another method the Company specifies in writing.

(e)  Fees: none. The Test Scene is unpaid (see Section 16).

Any changes to the scope of the Test Scene shall be agreed in writing by the Parties.
