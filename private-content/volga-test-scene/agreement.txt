This Agreement is made between Blendtobases OÜ, trading as BlendXR, a private limited company registered in Estonia with its registered office at Pärnu mnt 20, 10141 Tallinn, Estonia ("BlendXR"), and the company named in the signature block below (the "Contractor"). It takes effect when the Contractor signs it (the "Effective Date"). BlendXR has signed it before it is presented to the Contractor.

1. The Test. BlendXR invites the Contractor to build a single VR test scene as described in BlendXR's written brief titled "BlendXR x Volga — Test Scene Brief", made available through BlendXR's access-controlled website, together with any changes the parties agree in writing, including by email (the "Brief"). This work is the "Test". The Test is unpaid. No fee or expense is payable and the Contractor will not invoice BlendXR for it. Neither party is obliged to enter into any further engagement, and nothing in this Agreement creates exclusivity or a commitment to award or accept future work. Any paid work will be governed by a separate written agreement.

2. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information BlendXR discloses to the Contractor in connection with the Test, in any form, including the Brief; BlendXR's scenarios, hazard sequences, user flows, observation and cue logic (including the highlight pulse treatment), interaction logic, session data structures, workflow, techniques and art direction; files and assets BlendXR supplies or gives access to; the identity of BlendXR's clients and prospects; the fact and content of the parties' discussions; and all Test Work (defined in Section 4). It does not include information the Contractor can show (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Contractor, (b) was known to the Contractor before disclosure without a duty of confidence, (c) was independently developed by the Contractor without use of or reference to Confidential Information, or (d) was lawfully received from a third party free of any duty of confidence. Exclusions (b) and (c) do not apply to Test Work.

3. Non-disclosure and use. The Contractor will keep Confidential Information strictly confidential, will use it only to perform the Test, and will not disclose it to anyone, including by any public, social media or messaging channel, without BlendXR's prior written consent. The Contractor may share it only with its own employees and contractors who need it to perform the Test and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. The Contractor is responsible for any breach by those people. If disclosure is required by law or court order, the Contractor will give BlendXR prompt prior notice where legally permitted and disclose only what is required.

4. Ownership of Test Work. "Test Work" means everything the Contractor or its personnel create for the Test, including the scene, builds and APKs, the source project, Blueprints, code, materials, assets created for the Test, documentation and session data files. The Contractor hereby assigns to BlendXR all right, title and interest, including all intellectual property rights, in the Test Work, with effect from its creation and whether or not it is delivered. The assignment is worldwide, for the full term of those rights, covers all forms of use, and does not lapse if BlendXR does not use the Test Work. No royalty or other payment is due for it. To the extent moral rights cannot be assigned, the Contractor waives them and will not assert them against BlendXR. The Contractor confirms that its personnel who work on the Test have assigned, or will assign, their rights in their work to the Contractor so that this assignment is effective.

5. Pre-existing materials. The Contractor keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, code, know-how and general skills. If any pre-existing material of the Contractor is included in the Test Work, the Contractor grants BlendXR a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable licence to use, modify and distribute it as part of the Test Work. The Contractor will list every third-party asset in the Test Work (name, source and licence) as the Brief requires, and will not include any third-party material whose licence prevents BlendXR from using it.

6. No portfolio or promotional use. The Contractor will not display, publish, demonstrate or describe the Test Work, the Brief or any Confidential Information in any portfolio, website, showreel, case study, social media post, pitch, client presentation or other promotional material, and will not publicly state that it has worked on this project for BlendXR, without BlendXR's prior written consent, which BlendXR may withhold at its discretion.

7. Non-replication. During the term of this Agreement and for three (3) years after it ends, the Contractor will not, for itself or any third party, use the Test Work, the Brief or any Confidential Information, in whole or in part, to create any scene, product or experience, including by replicating or adapting BlendXR's scenarios, hazard sequences, user flows, observation and cue logic, highlight pulse treatment or session data structure. This Section does not restrict work the Contractor develops independently without use of or reference to the Test Work or Confidential Information.

8. Supplied assets and accounts. The Contractor will use files and assets supplied by BlendXR, and any third-party assets accessed through BlendXR's accounts (including Fab), only to perform the Test and in line with their licence terms, and will not share, resell or reuse them. Where BlendXR gives temporary access to one of its accounts, the Contractor will use it only to obtain the items BlendXR specifies and will make no other downloads, purchases or changes.

9. Delivery, return and deletion. The Contractor will deliver the Test Work, including the complete source project, through the method set out in the Brief. Delivery is not a condition of the assignment in Section 4. When the Test ends, or earlier on BlendXR's request, the Contractor will within seven (7) days permanently delete all Confidential Information, supplied assets and copies of the Test Work in its possession or control, except where retention is required by law, and will confirm the deletion to BlendXR in writing, which may be by email.

10. Term. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years unless either party ends it earlier by seven (7) days' written notice. Confidentiality obligations continue for three (3) years after it ends, and for as long as the information remains confidential in the case of Test Work and trade secrets. Sections 4 to 7, 9, 11 and 12 survive the end of this Agreement.

11. Remedies. The Contractor acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause BlendXR irreparable harm for which damages would not be adequate, and that BlendXR may seek an injunction or other equitable relief, without posting security, in addition to any other remedy.

12. Governing law and disputes. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of or relating to it will be resolved by arbitration before a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with its seat in New Delhi and conducted in English. Either party may seek interim relief, including an injunction, from any court of competent jurisdiction.

13. Authority. The individual signing for the Contractor confirms that they are authorised to bind the Contractor and that the company name given below is accurate. If it is not, or if the Contractor is not a registered legal entity, that individual is also personally bound by this Agreement, jointly and severally with the Contractor.

14. General. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter. It may be amended only in writing agreed by both parties, which may be by email. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in force. A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it. BlendXR may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or a successor to its business. The Contractor may not assign it or subcontract the Test without BlendXR's prior written consent. This Agreement may be executed electronically and in counterparts, and electronic signatures are binding.
